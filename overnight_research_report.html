1. Executive Summary

Refactored the legacy can_kpi + can_interactive_plot and UDP_KPI + intplot_kpi stacks into a unified 5-layer src/can_inplot/ / src/udp_inplot/ architecture with ZeroMQ transport. Researched and numerically verified 8 equation families (KL/JS/Wasserstein divergence, continuous-discrete Kalman noise, EKF/UKF and divergence-gated association). Key result: divergence-gated association suppresses radar false alarms under 50% specular clutter while Mahalanobis gating degrades — RMSE delta 6.8%; UKF tracks nonlinear polar measurements at RMSE 0.317 m (EKF: 0.333 m). Pipeline throughput 814.8 FPS at 300 scans with 64 detections/scan.
